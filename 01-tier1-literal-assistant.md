# Tier 1: The Literal Assistant

## Instructions for "Make me breakfast"

1. Go to the kitchen and stand in front of the fridge.
2. Open the fridge door and take out 2 eggs from the egg tray. Close the fridge door.
3. Place the eggs on the counter.
4. Take a non-stick frying pan from the cupboard under the stove and put it on the front left burner.
5. Take a small bowl from the shelf, crack both eggs into it, and throw the shells in the bin.
6. Add a pinch of salt and beat the eggs with a fork for 20 seconds.
7. Turn the burner to medium heat and add 1 tablespoon of cooking oil to the pan.
8. Wait 1 minute for the pan to heat up.
9. Pour the eggs into the pan.
10. Stir gently every 10 seconds until no liquid egg is visible, about 2 minutes.
11. Turn off the burner.
12. Slide the eggs onto a plate, put the plate on the dining table, and place a fork next to it.
13. The task is finished when the plate is on the table, the burner is off, and the pan is in the sink.

## Assumptions I made in the original instruction

- "Breakfast" means eggs. It could be bread, cereal, or rice.
- It knows where the kitchen, fridge, and pan are.
- It knows how much is "enough" food.
- It knows how to cook and when food is "done."
- It knows where "me" is and where to serve the food.
- It knows to turn off the stove and clean up afterward.
